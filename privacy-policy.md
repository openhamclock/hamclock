# Privacy Policy - HamClock for Roku

_Last updated: 10/9/2026_

This policy describes the Roku channel "HamClock" (the "channel"), published by openhamclock.

## What the channel does

The channel shows the screen of a HamClock that you run yourself and lets you send taps to it with your Roku
remote. It connects only to the network address you type in.

## What information is collected

None. The channel does not collect, store, transmit or sell personal information. It has no accounts,
analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What is stored on your device

The channel saves one setting, the HamClock address you entered (for example 192.168.1.20), in your Roku's
local storage so you do not have to type it again. This setting never leaves your Roku. Removing the channel
deletes it.

## Network traffic

The channel opens a connection from your Roku to the HamClock address you provided, normally on your own home
network. It exchanges the screen image and your taps with that device. The channel does not send data to the
developer or to any other third party.

## Roku

The channel runs on the Roku platform. Roku may collect its own information about your device and how you use
it, as described in Roku's privacy policy. The channel does not send anything to Roku beyond what the platform
itself requires to run an app.

## Third parties

The channel uses no third-party SDKs, libraries, analytics or advertising services. It is an independent
channel and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the author of HamClock. HamClock itself is separate software
that you run; its privacy practices are its own.

## Children

The channel is a utility for amateur radio hobbyists and is not directed at children. It collects no
information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
